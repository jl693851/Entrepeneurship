      How the rating works
      
        
        
          
      • Each of the five dimensions is scored 0 (low risk) to 3 (high risk). “I don’t know” scores 3 — not knowing is itself the risk.
        • 
          
      • The total (0–15) is weighted by where the output ends up (×0.8 personal … ×1.4 decisions about people/money/health/law).
        • 
          
      • Bands: under 4 Low · under 8 Moderate · under 11 High · 11+ Critical.
        • 
          
      • Red-flag rules override the average, e.g. sensitive data + no reviewer is at least High, no matter how good the rest looks.
        • 
        
      
        
      Known limits
      
        
        
          
      • It rates your self-reported answers. Optimistic answers produce optimistic ratings.
        • 
          
      • Keyword detection for context hints is crude; it can miss or over-flag.
        • 
          
      • It is a thinking exercise, not a compliance or legal assessment (e.g. GDPR, EU AI Act).
