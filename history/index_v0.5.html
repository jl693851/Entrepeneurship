        How the rating works
        
        
          
          
        • Each of the five areas is scored from 0 (low risk) to 3 (high risk). “I don’t know” scores 3, because not knowing is itself the risk.
          • 
          
        • The total (0–15) is weighted by where the output ends up, from ×0.8 for personal use to ×1.4 for decisions about people, money, health or law.
          • 
          
        • Bands: under 4 is Low, under 8 Moderate, under 11 High, and 11 or more Critical.
          • 
          
        • Red-flag rules override the total. For example, sensitive data with no reviewer is at least High, however good the rest looks.
          • 
        
        
        
        Known limits
        
        
          
          
        • It rates your own answers. Optimistic answers produce optimistic ratings.
          • 
          
        • The context hints come from simple keyword matching. They can miss things or over-flag.
          • 
          
        • It is a thinking exercise, not a compliance or legal assessment (for example under GDPR or the EU AI Act).
          • 
        
        
        
        Privacy
        
        
          
          
        • The check runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent while you answer.
          • 
          
        • If you send feedback, your plan description, scores and comments are stored by the ClearAI team for evaluating the prototype.
